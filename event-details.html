EVENT INFORMATION

EVENT GUIDELINES

Please read the following guidelines and judging criteria carefully before participating in the event.

01

Event Guidelines & Instructions

01

Presentation Duration

Each team will get 5–7 minutes for their presentation.

02

PPT Guidelines

The PPT must not be created using AI tools. The provided PPT template is mandatory and must be followed.

03

ID Card

All participants must carry the ID card of their respective college.

04

Reporting Time

Participants must report to the venue at least 15 minutes before their allotted time. Late entry may result in deduction of points.

05

Entry QR Code

The QR code is mandatory for event entry.

06

Movement Within Premises

Participants are not permitted to roam around the premises and must remain only in their allotted classrooms.

07

Prototype Backup

Teams must keep a backup copy of their developed prototype to avoid loss of work due to technical issues.

08

Cleanliness

A ₹50 fine will be imposed for littering within the premises.

09

Discipline

Any form of misbehavior or misconduct may lead to expulsion from the event.

02

Judging Criteria

Participants will be evaluated based on:

01

Novelty

Originality and uniqueness of the idea.

02

Real-World Impact

Practical relevance and potential impact of the proposed solution.

03

Presentation Skills

Clarity, confidence, communication, and effective presentation in English.
